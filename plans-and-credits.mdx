Buildpad offers two plans: Pro and Max. Both come with a 3-day free trial so you can try the full experience before committing. Each plan includes monthly credits that you use as you work.

## Plans

**Pro** — &#36;39/month
- 200 credits per month
- Enough for ~5 projects
- Unused credits roll over
- Email support

**Max** — &#36;85/month
- 500 credits per month
- Enough for ~12 projects
- Unused credits roll over
- Premium support

All plans include every feature. Plans only differ in the amount of credits you receive each month.

## Free trial

New users get a 3-day free trial on both Pro and Max. During the trial you receive 10 credits per day to explore the platform. Your subscription starts automatically when the trial ends.

## How credits work

Credits reflect the AI processing required for each task. Different tasks use different amounts. A simple back-and-forth message uses fewer credits than [research](/research) or [ultraplanning](/ultraplan), which process more information and take longer to complete.

Your credit balance updates in real-time as you work. You can see your current balance on the dashboard. Hover over it to see your plan details and when your credits reset.

## Credit rollovers

Pro and Max subscribers keep unused credits. They roll over automatically at the end of each billing cycle as long as you keep an active subscription.

Rolled over credits are available for one month.

**Cancellation**: When you cancel a paid subscription, all unused and rolled over credits expire at the end of your current billing period. They do not carry over.

## Buying additional credits

Pro and Max subscribers can purchase additional credit packs anytime:

| Credits | Price |
|---------|-------|
| 100 | &#36;20 |
| 500 | &#36;100 |

To buy credits, hover over your credit balance on the dashboard and select **Add credits**. Purchased credits roll over for one month.
