aicofounder offers three plans: Free, Pro, and Max. Each plan includes monthly credits that you use as you work.

## Plans

**Free** — $0/month
- Free usage to get started
- All features included

**Pro** — $20/month or $16/month billed annually
- 100 credits per month
- Enough for ~3 projects
- Unused credits roll over
- Email support

**Max** — $80/month or $64/month billed annually
- 500 credits per month
- Enough for ~15 projects
- Unused credits roll over
- Premium support

All plans include every feature. Plans only differ in the amount of credits you receive each month.

## Annual billing

Pro and Max are available with annual billing at a discounted rate. Annual plans are billed once per year but credits are still delivered monthly — you receive your monthly allocation at the start of each month, just like monthly subscribers.

| Plan | Monthly | Annual (per month) | Annual savings |
|------|---------|-------------------|----------------|
| Pro | $20/mo | $16/mo | Save 20% |
| Max | $80/mo | $64/mo | Save $192/yr |

## How credits work

Credits reflect the AI processing required for each task. Different tasks use different amounts. A simple back-and-forth message uses fewer credits than [deep research](/deep-research) or [ultraplanning](/ultraplan), which process more information and take longer to complete.

Your credit balance updates in real-time as you work. You can see your current balance on the dashboard. Hover over it to see your plan details and when your credits reset.

## Monthly reset

Credits reset on your signup anniversary each month. If you signed up on the 15th, your credits reset to your plan amount on the 15th of each month.

## Credit rollovers

Pro and Max subscribers keep unused credits. They roll over automatically at the end of each billing cycle as long as you keep an active subscription.

- **Monthly plans**: Rolled over credits are available for one month.
- **Annual plans**: Rolled over credits accumulate throughout your annual billing period, up to a maximum of 12 times your monthly allocation.

Free plan credits do not roll over.

**Cancellation**: When you cancel a paid subscription, all unused and rolled over credits expire at the end of your current billing period. They do not carry over.

## Buying additional credits

Pro and Max subscribers can purchase additional credit packs anytime:

| Credits | Price | Discount |
|---------|-------|----------|
| 250 | $50 | — |
| 500 | $90 | Save 10% |

To buy credits, hover over your credit balance on the dashboard and select **Add credits**. Purchased credits roll over for one month.
